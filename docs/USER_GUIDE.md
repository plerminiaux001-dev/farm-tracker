# 🧑‍🌾 Farm Tracker — Simple Guide & Instructions

Welcome to **Farm Tracker**! This guide is designed to be simple and easy to follow so anyone can start planning crops, tracking garden beds, and logging harvests right away.

---

## 📱 Quick Setup: Add to Phone Home Screen

You can use Farm Tracker like a regular mobile app on any smartphone (iPhone, iPad, Android, or Google Pixel):

### On Android / Google Pixel (Chrome):
1. Open the website in Google Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Farm Tracker will now appear on your home screen like a normal app and works offline in the garden!

### On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
1. Open the website in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share button** (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.

---

## 🧭 The 6 Main Tabs (At a Glance)

At the top of your screen (or bottom bar on your phone), you'll see 6 tabs:

| Tab | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **📋 Tasks** | Your daily/weekly to-do list: tells you what seeds to sow, what to plant outside, and what's ready to harvest. |
| **📅 Timeline** | Visual schedule cards and a 12-month calendar showing when every crop is growing and when it will be ready. |
| **🥕 Crops** | A library of over 230+ vegetables and varieties with days to harvest, seed spacing, and planting windows. |
| **📍 Beds** | Your garden map: see which crops are growing in which bed, row, or greenhouse. |
| **📜 History** | A searchable logbook of past garden records and notes from previous years. |
| **⚙️ Settings** | Your frost dates, beds list, Google Sheets cloud sync, and backup tools. |

## 🌿 Step-by-Step: How to Use Farm Tracker

### Step 1: Check Your Frost Dates (First Time Only)
1. Tap the **⚙️ Settings** tab.
2. Check your **Spring Last Frost Date** (e.g. `May 15`) and **Fall First Frost Date** (e.g. `October 15`).
3. Tap **💾 Save Settings**.
> *Farm Tracker automatically uses these dates to calculate the exact week you should start seeds indoors, plant them outside, and expect your harvest.*

---

### Step 2: Set Up Your Garden Beds
1. Tap the **📍 Beds** tab.
2. Tap the blue **➕ Add Bed** button.
3. Type the name of your bed, row, or plot (e.g. `Bed 1`, `Greenhouse Row 2`, `Tomato Patch`, `North Raised Bed`) and tap **Add Bed**.
4. *To rename or remove a bed:* Tap the ✏️ pencil icon or 🗑️ trash icon right on any bed card.

---

### Step 3: Plan Your Crops for the Season
1. Tap the **🥕 Crops** tab.
2. Use the search bar at the top or choose a category (e.g. type `Tomato`, `Carrot`, `Kale`, or `Beans`).
3. Find the variety you want to plant and tap **➕ Plan Sowing**.
4. A small window will appear with calculated dates:
   - **Sowing Method**: Choose *Indoor Seed Starting* or *Direct Sowing Outside*.
   - **Dates**: Farm Tracker suggests the best dates automatically, but you can change them if you prefer.
   - **Target Quantity**: How many seeds or plants (e.g. `24`).
   - **Assigned Bed**: Pick which garden bed this crop will go into.
5. Tap **Add to Schedule**.

---

### Step 4: Work in the Garden (Completing & Logging Tasks)
When you're out in the garden and ready to take action:
1. Tap the **📋 Tasks** tab.
2. You will see what is due to be sowed, planted, or harvested.
3. When you finish a task, tap the green **✓ Action** button on the card:
   - **✓ Sow Seeds**: Confirms you started the seeds.
   - **✓ Plant Out**: Confirms you transplanted seedlings into the garden bed.
   - **✓ Harvest**: Lets you record pounds harvested and notes (e.g. "Great flavor, huge yield!").
4. Tap **Save Log**.

---

### Step 5: Viewing Your Season Timeline
1. Tap the **📅 Timeline** tab.
2. **📱 Schedule Cards**: Shows each crop broken down into a 3-step timeline:
   - 🟣 **1. Indoor Sow Date**
   - 🟢 **2. Plant Out Date**
   - 🟠 **3. Harvest Window**
3. **📊 12-Mo Gantt**: Tap the *12-Mo Gantt* button at the top to see a full 12-month bird's-eye view bar chart of your entire garden season.
4. **Sorting**: Use the sort dropdown to sort by *Earliest Start Date*, *Harvest Date*, *Crop Name*, or *Garden Bed*.

---

### Step 6: Syncing with Google Sheets (Cloud Backup)
1. Tap the **☁️ Sync** button in the top header.
2. All your active crop plans and harvest logs will be backed up directly to your Google Sheet for free!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

#### 💡 Can I add a vegetable variety that isn't in the list?
Yes! In the **🥕 Crops** tab, tap **➕ New Crop** in the top-right corner. Fill in the vegetable name, variety, days to harvest (DTM), and tap **Save Variety**.

#### 💡 What if I sow seeds later than the suggested date?
No problem! Just tap the ✏️ pencil icon on that crop card in the **📋 Tasks** tab to update the date, or just tap **✓ Sow Seeds** on the day you actually planted them.

#### 💡 How do I start fresh or delete sample data?
Go to **⚙️ Settings**, scroll down to **🧹 Clear Data & Fresh Start**, and tap **🗑️ Clear All Planting Plans**.

#### 💡 Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes! Farm Tracker saves everything directly on your device. You can use it in the middle of the garden with no Wi-Fi or cellular service. When you get back inside, tap **☁️ Sync** to backup to Google Sheets.
